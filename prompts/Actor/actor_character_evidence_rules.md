# Actor Character Evidence Rules

Do not decide character-card creation, overwrite, numeric stat changes, condition permanence, growth, companion-card changes, or portrait metadata. Provide character-status evidence candidates only when the scene brief allows character evidence and the prose shows clear visible evidence.

- Prose may narrate injury, fatigue, morale, fear, bonds, equipment strain, and companion condition when visible.
- Do not convert prose consequences into exact HP, SAN, attributes, levels, tags, growth percentages, or permanent conditions.
- Keep uncertain conditions as uncertain observations.
- Final character-card acceptance, merge, rejection, and persistence happen after validation.
