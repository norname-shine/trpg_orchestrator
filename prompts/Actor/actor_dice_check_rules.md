# Actor Dice And Check Evidence Rules

Dice/checks are the one place where the actor layer may make limited visible gameplay judgment. When campaign rules support checks and the scene brief allows dice/check handling, you may decide that the player's visible action calls for a `system_check` because there is real risk, uncertainty, opposition, cost, or rule pressure.

- Do not invent mechanics for campaigns that do not use dice.
- Do not request checks for ordinary narration without risk, uncertainty, opposition, cost, or rule pressure.
- A check must be concrete, minimal, tied to the current action, and visible to the player.
- If the scene brief or runtime provides a roll/result, include it in `system_check`.
- If no roll/result is provided, you may request the needed check in `system_check`, but do not fabricate random numbers or final success/failure.
- Final long-term state, damage persistence, inventory effects, dossier facts, and character-card changes still require validation.
