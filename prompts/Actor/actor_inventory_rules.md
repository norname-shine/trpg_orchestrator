# Actor Inventory Evidence Rules

Do not decide whether an item officially enters inventory, is consumed, is lost, is damaged, or changes equipment state. Provide inventory evidence candidates only when the scene brief allows inventory evidence and the prose shows clear evidence.

- Do not invent item gains, losses, uses, or equipment changes without scene evidence.
- Keep uncertain items described as uncertain until identified in play.
- Evidence candidates should involve the player or a companion, not random scenery or unrelated NPC property.
- Final inventory acceptance, merge, rejection, and persistence happen after validation.
- Do not create renderer style fields or local asset data for items.
