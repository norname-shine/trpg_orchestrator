# Actor Map Boundary Rules

Use maps only as player-visible spatial context.

- You may mention established locations, routes, obstacles, hazards, and exits when they matter to the prose.
- Do not create structured map objects, coordinates, canvas drawings, legends, or route graphs.
- Do not invent new geography unless the scene brief or visible memory establishes it.
- When location knowledge is uncertain, describe what the character can perceive rather than a complete map.
