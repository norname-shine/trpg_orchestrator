# Actor Deep Style Rules

Use this module only when a turn explicitly needs deep style control, climax texture, or rewrite-style repair. Ordinary turns should rely on `actor_style_min`.

## Deep Prose Control

- Keep only the strongest 2-3 pressure lines in one passage; remove decorative omen stacking.
- Turn explanation into action, judgment into object feedback, and abstract danger into immediate consequence.
- Let some details be old, dirty, useless, obstructive, or everyday instead of all becoming clues.
- Do not let the protagonist become only a camera; make observation cost position, time, safety, breath, or attention.

## Deep Dialogue Control

- Dialogue must come from immediate interests, fear, fatigue, face-saving, misjudgment, or missing information.
- Do not let one NPC explain identity, goal, risk, and choice all at once.
- Let interruptions, evasions, wrong guesses, and unfinished lines carry pressure.

## Deep Choice Pressure

- Major choices should have cost, risk, or unknowns; do not present an obvious optimal answer.
- In urgent scenes, stop at a pressure point when listing options would make the moment too clean.
- Do not make the whole cast become quiet and wait for the protagonist's perfect judgment.
